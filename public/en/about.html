WHO WE ARE

About Raven Crow

Independent journalism on politics, economics, law and ideas. We separate what happened, what we think about it and what those who disagree say.

What we publish

  • NewsThe facts, with sources at the end of each story, in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
  • The Crow's ColumnOpinion essays on philosophy, faith and public life, published as signed opinion.
  • LibraryBooks to deepen the debate, organized by author.
  • VideosClips and commentary on YouTube and on Instagram.

How we work

  • Fact, analysis and counterpointEach story gives what happened, the Crow's reading and the strongest counterargument, in separate sections.
  • Sources firstWe publish only with a primary source or two independent news sources. A social media post is a lead, not confirmation.
  • Licensed photosWe use images cleared for use, with credit. With no cleared photo, the story runs without one. See the credits and licenses.
  • Technology in service of reportingWe use automation and artificial intelligence tools to monitor sources, draft and translate. The rules above apply to everything we publish.

Corrections

Did we get it wrong? Let us know via @ravencrowcc on Instagram with the story link and the passage in question. We fix the text and, when a change alters the meaning, we say so on the page itself.

Who pays the bills

The site is funded by ads and by voluntary support via Pix (Brazilian instant payment). No party, government or company pays for our coverage, and all content is free.
