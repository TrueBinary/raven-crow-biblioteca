Six prison officers arrested in probe into smuggling at Guarulhos prisons

Joint police and prosecutors' operation targets suspected smuggling of drugs and phones into prisons, with 16 arrest warrants and 21 search warrants across seven São Paulo municipalities.

What happened

On November 10, 2026, an operation led by São Paulo's Integrated Force to Combat Organized Crime (Ficco/SP), alongside the organized-crime unit of the São Paulo Public Prosecutor's Office (Gaeco), arrested six prison officers suspected of helping smuggle drugs, mobile phones and other prohibited items into prisons in Guarulhos, Greater São Paulo. According to information attributed to the Federal Police and reported independently by UOL and Metrópoles, courts issued 16 arrest warrants and 21 search warrants across São Paulo, Guarulhos, Potim, Mauá, Mogi das Cruzes, São José dos Campos and Bauru. Two additional prison officers were subject to searches. Investigators are also examining the alleged involvement of inmates, relatives, suppliers and people handling funds. São Paulo's prison police and Military Police supported the operation. The reported arrest count is preliminary and may change.

The Crow's analysis

The allegations raise concerns about internal prison security and oversight when the very officials responsible for custody are suspected of enabling contraband. Arrests and warrants are not convictions. Any criminal liability will depend on evidence examined by prosecutors and the courts. The operation targets alleged facilitators inside prisons as well as outside suppliers and financial intermediaries.

Counterpoint

Those investigated retain the presumption of innocence and the right to defend themselves. The reports reviewed do not include individual responses from every suspect. The archive photograph shows a different São Paulo prison and is not an image of the Guarulhos operation.
