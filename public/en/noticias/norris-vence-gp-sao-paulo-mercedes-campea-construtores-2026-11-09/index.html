Norris wins rain-hit São Paulo Grand Prix as Mercedes seals constructors' title

McLaren's British driver won at Interlagos on Sunday, November 8; Verstappen climbed from 21st to second, with Hadjar third.

What happened

Lando Norris won the Formula 1 São Paulo Grand Prix on Sunday, November 8, 2026, at the José Carlos Pace circuit in Interlagos. The McLaren driver started on pole and completed the 71-lap race in changing conditions, with rain early and late in the event, finishing 14.6 seconds ahead of Max Verstappen. The Red Bull driver recovered from 21st on the grid to finish second, while teammate Isack Hadjar came third. Mercedes drivers George Russell and Kimi Antonelli finished fourth and fifth, earning enough points to secure the team's ninth Formula 1 Constructors' Championship. Oscar Piastri placed sixth. According to Formula 1's official race report, this was Norris's third win of the season. The race featured tyre changes and strategy calls as track conditions shifted.

The Crow's analysis

Norris combined pace and timely strategy as rain returned near the finish. Verstappen's recovery from 21st to second was another highlight. Mercedes secured the constructors' championship through the combined points scored by Russell and Antonelli despite not winning the race. The changing conditions underlined how tyre management and weather decisions can reshape the order at Interlagos.

Counterpoint

Mercedes winning the constructors' championship does not mean one of its drivers won the race: Norris drove for McLaren. Reuters reported that Antonelli had already clinched the 2026 drivers' title in Mexico.
