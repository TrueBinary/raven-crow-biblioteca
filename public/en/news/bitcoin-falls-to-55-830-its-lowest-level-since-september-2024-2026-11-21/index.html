BITCOIN FALLS TO $55,830, ITS LOWEST LEVEL SINCE SEPTEMBER 2024

Bitcoin falls 5.10% to $55,830 amid a global sell-off in risk assets.

What happened

Bitcoin fell to $55,830 at 11:13 p.m. US Eastern time on November 20, 2026, amid a global sell-off in risk assets, Reuters reported. The news agency recorded a 5.10% decline at that time and said the price was its lowest since September 2024. CoinDesk separately reported that bitcoin had fallen below $57,000 on November 20 as selling spread across technology stocks and other assets. These figures are snapshots, not fixed prices for the rest of the trading day.

The Crow's analysis

The decline extended a series of losses coinciding with pressure on technology shares and artificial intelligence investments. Investors often reduce exposure to volatile assets when borrowing costs and uncertainty about future earnings rise. Breaking widely watched levels such as $60,000 and $57,000 may amplify volatility, but does not establish the direction of future prices. For Brazilian investors, dollar-denominated returns also depend on exchange rates and transaction costs.

Counterpoint

Reuters and CoinDesk document the price decline and broader risk aversion, but do not establish a single cause. Quotes differ across times and trading venues, and the observations do not justify a price forecast.
