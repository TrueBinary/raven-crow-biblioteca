STORMS DAMAGE 250 HOMES AND AFFECT 907 PEOPLE IN PARANÁ, BRAZIL

Civil Defense reports 18 displaced residents and four destroyed buildings; nearly 49,000 electricity customers were without power.

What happened

Storms affecting Paraná state in southern Brazil since Friday, November 20, 2026, damaged homes and disrupted electricity supplies across several municipalities, according to a Civil Defense update issued on Sunday, November 22. According to Civil Defense figures reported by Agência Brasil, 250 homes were damaged, four buildings destroyed, 18 people displaced and 907 people affected. Paraná electricity utility Companhia Paranaense de Energia (Copel), as cited by Agência Brasil, reported 48,837 customers without power at 4:09 p.m. on Sunday, including 23,099 in western Paraná, with 512 crews working on restoration. Civil Defense recorded windstorms in nine municipalities, hail in São José dos Pinhais and a tornado in Manoel Ribas.

The Crow's analysis

These figures describe different impacts and should not be added together: 'affected' includes people experiencing disruptions to essential services, while 'displaced' refers to those forced to leave their homes. The concentration of power outages in western Paraná shows the disruption extends beyond properties physically damaged. Agência Brasil reported that low pressure over northern Paraguay and a stationary front over Paraná could sustain unstable weather into the early hours of Monday, November 23. Updated power-restoration and Civil Defense reports will be important for residents.

Counterpoint

The figures are preliminary and may change as local authorities complete their assessments. The outage count is a snapshot from 4:09 p.m. on November 22, not a permanent total. The tornado reported in Manoel Ribas does not mean the same weather phenomenon struck every affected municipality.
