Canada pledges up to C$560 million to Brazil-led tropical forest fund

Mark Carney announced the four-year commitment to Lula in Manila; disbursements remain ahead.

What happened

On November 15, 2026, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced an initial contribution of up to C$560 million over four years to the Tropical Forest Forever Facility (TFFF), co-led by Brazil and Norway. He met Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on the sidelines of the ASEAN summit in Manila, Philippines. Under the proposed mechanism, at least 20% of conservation payments would reach Indigenous Peoples and local communities. Reuters valued the pledge's ceiling at about US$392.5 million.

The Crow's analysis

The pledge strengthens international support for a mechanism designed to reward verified tropical forest conservation, using public and private investment. Implementation still depends on fundraising, governance and measurable outcomes.

Counterpoint

This is a maximum four-year pledge, not a fully disbursed payment. The fund's ambition to protect more than one billion hectares once operational is not an accomplished result of this announcement.
