FEDERAL POLICE DELIVER REPORTS TO FACHIN ON JUSTICES MENTIONED IN MASTER CASE

Documents remain confidential; Brazil's Supreme Court president is expected to refer them to prosecutors.

What happened

On the evening of November 18, 2026, in Brasília, Federal Police (PF) director-general Andrei Rodrigues delivered reports to Edson Fachin, president of Brazil's Supreme Federal Court (STF), concerning references to justices found in the Banco Master investigation. According to Agência Brasil, Fachin requested a contextual, descriptive account of references found on former banker Daniel Vorcaro's phone. The documents remain confidential. Fachin is expected to send them to the Prosecutor General's Office (PGR) for an assessment of possible next steps.

The Crow's analysis

Delivery of the reports completes a documentary step, not an investigation or trial of any justice. Identifying a name in messages, assessing grounds for investigative measures and authorizing a formal investigation are distinct steps. Context matters: being mentioned by another person is not evidence of wrongdoing. The PGR's assessment and subsequent court decisions will determine whether there are procedural consequences.

Counterpoint

The reports have not been made public, so their contents cannot be treated as proof of a crime by any justice. During a Senate hearing on November 18, Rodrigues attributed the removal of two officers from leadership positions to a breakdown of trust and reports he said exceeded instructions. This is his account, not an independent finding about the officers' conduct. The balance between transparency and investigative confidentiality remains unresolved.
