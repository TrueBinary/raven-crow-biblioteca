FIFA announces December 1 ticket sales launch for 2027 Women's World Cup in Brazil

The first sales window runs December 1–8, with packages featuring tickets from R$ 30 per match.

What happened

On November 13, 2026, FIFA announced that the first ticket sales window for the 2027 Women's World Cup in Brazil will run from December 1 at 10 a.m. local time through December 8, ahead of the December 11 draw. Three multi-match packages will be offered: Brazil Team Pass for Brazil's three group games, Arena Pass for matches at a chosen stadium, and Flex Pass for three eligible games. Some packages feature category 3 tickets starting at R$ 30 per match, depending on availability and the selected fixtures. Sales will take place exclusively through FIFA's official ticket website.

The Crow's analysis

The announcement gives fans time to prepare for the first Women's World Cup hosted in South America. The initial phase focuses on multi-match packages rather than unrestricted single-match sales. FIFA also announced legally mandated half-price options and accessible seating. Advertised entry prices do not apply to every seat or guarantee availability; allocations will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis.

Counterpoint

A R$ 30 entry price makes headlines, but it is not a guaranteed price for every match. Fans should check full package costs, eligibility rules and travel expenses, and avoid unauthorized sellers.
