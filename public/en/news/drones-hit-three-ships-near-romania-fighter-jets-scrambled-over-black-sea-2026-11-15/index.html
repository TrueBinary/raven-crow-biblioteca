DRONES HIT THREE SHIPS NEAR ROMANIA; FIGHTER JETS SCRAMBLED OVER BLACK SEA

The November 14 strikes near the Sulina Canal caused fires but no casualties, Romanian officials said. The drones' origin remains unidentified.

What happened

Three commercial vessels — Evia Petrol V, Marsol and Ali Najafov — were struck by drones near Romania's Sulina Canal on the night of November 14, 2026, according to statements by Prime Minister Luca Niculescu and Defence Minister Ștefan-Radu Oprea reported on November 15. Crews contained the fires and no casualties were reported. The first two vessels were approximately four and five nautical miles southeast of Sulina, within Romanian territorial waters. Romanian fighter jets were scrambled twice after aerial targets were detected. A separate cargo ship was struck on Friday, November 13, also without casualties. Romanian authorities have not identified who launched the drones.

The Crow's analysis

The Sulina Canal links the Danube to the Black Sea and serves as an important export route for Ukraine. Attacks on merchant shipping near NATO member Romania heighten risks to civilian navigation and increase pressure for surveillance, patrols and counter-drone protection. Oprea said he would raise the matter with European defence counterparts.

Counterpoint

The war's proximity does not establish responsibility for each individual strike. Romania links the deterioration of regional security to Russia's war against Ukraine, but attribution of these particular drone attacks still requires evidence. Protecting crews and commercial shipping remains the immediate priority.
