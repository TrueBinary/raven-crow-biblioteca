BRAZIL OPENS CHECKS FOR 223,793 INCOME TAX REFUNDS; PAYMENT DUE NOVEMBER 30

Brazil opens checks for 223,793 tax refunds worth R$666.3 million, with payment scheduled for November 30.

What happened

Brazil's Federal Revenue Service opened nationwide checks for its November residual Personal Income Tax (IRPF) refunds at 9 a.m. on November 23, 2026. According to the agency's official statement issued in Brasília, the batch includes 223,793 refunds totalling R$666,285,077.46, with bank transfers scheduled for November 30, 2026. The Federal Revenue Service says R$382,972,486.59 is earmarked for legally prioritised taxpayers; the batch covers late returns, resolved filing issues and residual refunds from earlier years. The opening of the online check does not mean funds have already been deposited.

The Crow's analysis

Taxpayers can check the result through the Federal Revenue Service website under My Income Tax > Check my refund or through its official app. Compared with October's residual batch of 184,442 refunds totalling R$531,601,793.51, November has 39,351 more refunds and roughly R$134.7 million more in planned payments. Legal priority covers qualifying older people, people with disabilities or serious illnesses, and teachers; a separate administrative priority applies to pre-filled returns and/or Pix payments. Taxpayers with outstanding filing issues can check their records and submit corrections through the e-CAC portal.

Counterpoint

The announcement does not establish that every transfer will succeed on November 30. Incorrect bank details can prevent payment, in which case the agency allows rescheduling through Banco do Brasil within one year of the first attempt. Taxpayers should use official channels rather than links sent by third parties.
