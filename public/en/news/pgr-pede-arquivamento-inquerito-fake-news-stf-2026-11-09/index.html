Brazil's prosecutor-general asks Supreme Court to close seven-year 'fake news' inquiry

Paulo Gonet's recommendation was sent to Supreme Court president Edson Fachin, who must decide whether to formally close the 2019 investigation.

What happened

Brazil's Procuradoria-Geral da República (PGR, Office of the Prosecutor-General) requested on Monday, November 9, 2026, that the Supremo Tribunal Federal (STF, Brazil's Federal Supreme Court) close the so-called fake news inquiry opened in 2019. According to Folha de S.Paulo and UOL, Prosecutor-General Paulo Gonet submitted his recommendation to Chief Justice Edson Fachin, who took charge of the case in September. Previously overseen by Justice Alexandre de Moraes, the inquiry was launched to investigate threats, insults and attacks against STF justices. It became controversial over its duration, secrecy and manner of initiation. Folha reports that the original case file contains approximately 12,000 pages and that eight indictments were directly related to its initial scope. Gonet's recommendation is not a final closure order; Fachin must consider it and issue a decision.

The Crow's analysis

The PGR's request marks a further institutional step toward ending an investigation that has lasted more than seven years and prompted debate over the STF's investigative powers, transparency and protection of its members. A decision to close the inquiry would not, by itself, establish that every measure taken was unlawful or automatically end separate investigations or criminal cases. The reasoning and precise scope of any court order will be crucial to assessing its legal and political effects.

Counterpoint

The STF upheld the inquiry's creation in 2020, although critics questioned the assignment of the case without a random draw and the concentration of institutional roles. Supporters argued that the investigation was needed in response to threats against judges and democratic institutions. The prosecutor-general's submission is a recommendation, not a completed judicial closure.
