MARC MÁRQUEZ CLINCHES EIGHTH MOTOGP TITLE WITH EIGHTH PLACE IN PORTUGAL

The Spaniard seals the world championship in Portimão with one round to spare as Marco Bezzecchi wins the race.

What happened

Spanish Ducati Lenovo Team rider Marc Márquez secured his eighth premier-class MotoGP world championship on Sunday, November 22, 2026, by finishing eighth at the Portuguese Grand Prix in Portimão. According to MotoGP's official report, the title was decided at round 21 of 22 despite Márquez finishing off the podium. MotoGP also reports that he had trailed Marco Bezzecchi by 102 points after round seven; in Portugal, Bezzecchi won ahead of Jorge Martín and Raúl Fernández.

The Crow's analysis

The race result and championship result tell different stories: Aprilia swept the podium, but Márquez's eighth place was enough to make his points lead unassailable. According to MotoGP, his eighth premier-class title draws him level with Giacomo Agostini. Overcoming a 102-point deficit illustrates the importance of season-long consistency rather than a single race result. Valencia remains relevant to other standings, not the riders' title.

Counterpoint

Winning the riders' championship does not mean Ducati won the Portuguese Grand Prix; Bezzecchi and Aprilia swept the podium. The historical comparison concerns premier-class titles, not championships across all motorcycle racing classes.
