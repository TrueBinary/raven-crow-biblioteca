ENEM 2026DEADLINE TO REQUEST A MAKEUP EXAM IS FRIDAY AT 11:59 PM

Brazil's Inep requires applications by November 20; makeup tests are scheduled for December 15 and 16.

What happened

Brazil's National Institute for Educational Studies and Research (Inep) announced on Thursday, November 19, 2026, that requests to retake the National High School Exam (Enem) must be submitted by Friday, November 20, at 11:59 p.m. Brasília time. Applicants must use the official Participant Page and provide supporting documents. Eligible circumstances include verified logistical failures, natural disasters that disrupted testing, and infectious diseases specified in the exam rules. Makeup exams are scheduled for December 15 and 16.

The Crow's analysis

The deadline matters because it expires tomorrow, but the makeup exam is not an unrestricted second chance for absentees. Candidates must document a qualifying circumstance. Regular exams took place on November 8 and 15; those affected on only one day request a makeup for that day's tests alone. The practical step is to visit https://enem.inep.gov.br/participante and submit the required evidence before the deadline. Inep reported 5.05 million confirmed registrations for the 2026 exam.

Counterpoint

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval: Inep reviews the evidence. Rain without proven disruption, personal absence or lateness alone do not establish eligibility. Candidates should check their application status and the official rules.
