BRAZIL'S UNEMPLOYMENT FALLS TO 5.1%, MATCHING RECORD LOW

IBGE reports 103.7 million employed people and average real earnings of R$3,812 in the quarter through October.

What happened

On November 27, 2026, the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) reported that Brazil's unemployment rate was 5.1% from August through October, down from 5.3% in the previous quarter. According to the Continuous National Household Sample Survey (PNAD), 103.7 million people were employed and 5.6 million were unemployed. Average usual real earnings reached R$3,812, up 3.5% year on year.

The Crow's analysis

Lower unemployment and increased purchasing power support household consumption. The survey also shows employment growth in transport and activities including education and health. For businesses, earnings trends matter for demand, although outcomes vary across sectors.

Counterpoint

The record does not mean full employment: labor underutilization remained at 12.7%, according to IBGE. Total employment was statistically stable compared with the previous quarter, as was formal private-sector employment. The data do not guarantee continued improvement in coming months.
