VERSTAPPEN WINS LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX BY 0.639 SECONDS AFTER 50 LAPS

Max Verstappen won in Las Vegas ahead of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, according to Formula 1's official classification.

What happened

Red Bull Racing driver Max Verstappen won the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix held on November 21, 2026, local time, on the Las Vegas street circuit in the United States. According to Formula 1's official results, Verstappen completed 50 laps in 1:27:39.624 and finished 0.639 seconds ahead of McLaren's Lando Norris; Oscar Piastri placed third, 1.529 seconds behind. The organizer reported 35 changes of lead, with Mercedes drivers George Russell and Kimi Antonelli also challenging at the front.

The Crow's analysis

The margin of less than one second illustrates why this was far from a straightforward pole-to-flag victory. Formula 1's race account describes repeated passes and lead changes shaped by slipstreaming and energy management. Russell fell to ninth after a late braking error, while Antonelli finished fourth. McLaren secured second and third despite Mercedes repeatedly threatening Red Bull during the race.

Counterpoint

These positions reflect the official classification consulted after the race; any subsequent stewards' decisions should be checked against the series' documentation.
