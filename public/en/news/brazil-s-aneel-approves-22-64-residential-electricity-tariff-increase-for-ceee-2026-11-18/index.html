BRAZIL'S ANEEL APPROVES 22.64% RESIDENTIAL ELECTRICITY TARIFF INCREASE FOR CEEE-D IN RIO GRANDE DO SUL

Approved on November 17, the revised rates take effect November 22; the average impact is 20.16% across 2.03 million customer units.

What happened

Brazil's National Electric Energy Agency (Aneel) approved a periodic tariff review for CEEE-D on November 17, 2026. The Equatorial-owned utility serves approximately 2.03 million customer units across 72 municipalities in Rio Grande do Sul. Effective November 22, residential B1 tariffs rise 22.64%, low-voltage tariffs increase 22.51% on average, high-voltage tariffs rise 12.28%, and the overall average effect is 20.16%. Aneel cited distribution costs, power purchases, sector charges and financial components.

The Crow's analysis

The 20.16% overall figure is a weighted average, not the rate applicable to each household; the approved residential B1 increase is 22.64%. For illustration, a R$100 tariff component fully subject to this rate would become R$122.64, excluding changes in usage, taxes, public-lighting charges and tariff flags. Aneel's October proposal projected a 22.31% average effect, 2.15 percentage points higher than the final decision. A periodic review also reassesses efficient distribution costs and regulatory parameters. The consultation and hearing received 19 contributions.

Counterpoint

The lower final percentage compared with the proposal does not mean electricity bills are falling. Actual bills depend on customer category, consumption, taxes and other charges; applying the percentage to every bill's total would be misleading.
