Banco do Brasil and KfW plan €100 million Amazon bioeconomy financing deal

Partnership announced at COP31 targets credit for producers and communities; the financing structure is still being finalized.

What happened

Banco do Brasil and Germany's development bank KfW announced a joint declaration on November 11, 2026, during COP31 to structure a €100 million financing operation for sustainable bioeconomy supply chains, with an emphasis on the Amazon. Banco do Brasil will identify and allocate eligible lending operations, while KfW will finance and monitor the project on behalf of German-Brazilian financial cooperation. Intended beneficiaries include small producers, family farmers, traditional communities, Indigenous peoples, cooperatives and small businesses. The announcement is not an immediate disbursement: the operation still needs to be structured. Agência Brasil reported further details on November 12.

The Crow's analysis

The proposed financing could improve access to credit for activities that generate income while protecting ecosystems, but the outcome will depend on final loan terms, beneficiary selection and implementation. The initiative follows a partnership announced at COP30 in 2025 involving €50 million in funding and technical assistance. Meaningful evaluation will require tracking actual disbursements, defaults, jobs and environmental outcomes rather than headline commitments.

Counterpoint

A €100 million announcement makes headlines, but money promised at a summit is not money in a farmer's account. The real test is who gets the loans, on what terms and whether bureaucracy lets the funds reach their intended recipients.
