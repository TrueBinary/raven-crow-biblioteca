FERNANDO ALONSO CONFIRMS 2027 DAKAR RETURN WITH ASTON MARTIN AND LUCAS MORAES

The two-time Formula 1 champion will race from January 9 to 23 alongside Sébastien Loeb and Brazilian driver Lucas Moraes in a Prodrive-run team.

What happened

On November 17, 2026, Aston Martin confirmed that Fernando Alonso will enter the 2027 Dakar Rally in its Prodrive-developed Dreadnought. The three-car team will feature Alonso, Sébastien Loeb and Brazil's Lucas Moraes. Alonso will be navigated by Marc Coma, Loeb by Fabian Lurquin and Moraes by Pablo Moreno. The 49th edition is scheduled for January 9–23, starting in Portugal before continuing mainly in Morocco. The announcement was corroborated by Formula 1 and Reuters.

The Crow's analysis

The project combines three different racing careers: Alonso is a two-time Formula 1 world champion, Loeb a nine-time world rally champion, and Moraes won the 2025 World Rally-Raid Championship. Moraes gives Brazilian fans a direct stake in Aston Martin's debut. Alonso finished 13th overall in his first Dakar in 2020 with Toyota. This time he will also prepare for the 2027 Formula 1 season. Rally-raid is an off-road endurance and navigation discipline, unlike F1 circuit racing.

Counterpoint

The line-up is official, but the new Dreadnought has not yet proved its race performance. Previous driver achievements do not guarantee a Dakar victory. The announced route and schedule remain subject to organizer changes.
