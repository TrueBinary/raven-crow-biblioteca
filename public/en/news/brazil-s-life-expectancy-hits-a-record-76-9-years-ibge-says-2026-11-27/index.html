BRAZIL'S LIFE EXPECTANCY HITS A RECORD 76.9 YEARS, IBGE SAYS

Life expectancy in 2025 rose by 3.1 months from a year earlier. The gap between women and men stayed at 6.6 years.

What happened

The Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) released Brazil's 2025 life tables on November 27, 2026. The institute put life expectancy at birth at 76.9 years, up from 76.6 in 2024. The increase was 3.1 months before rounding the yearly figures, with life expectancy reaching 80.1 years for women and 73.5 years for men.

The Crow's analysis

Longevity also improved for people who had already reached adulthood. According to figures reported by Agência Gov, a 60-year-old could expect to live another 22.7 years in 2025, up from 13.2 in 1940. Infant mortality fell from 146.6 to 12 deaths before the first birthday per thousand live births over the same period. Together, the measures show how lower mortality in infancy and at older ages lifted the national average.

Counterpoint

Life expectancy at birth is an estimate based on observed death rates at each age. It cannot predict an individual's lifespan. The national average also masks differences within the population: the gap between women and men stayed at 6.6 years even as both groups made gains in 2025.
