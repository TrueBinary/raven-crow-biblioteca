UAE vessel attacked in Persian Gulf as shipping risks persist near Hormuz

Operator confirms damage in UAE waters; cause, responsibility and any casualties have not been disclosed.

What happened

A vessel from the United Arab Emirates was attacked and damaged in UAE territorial waters in the Persian Gulf on the afternoon of Tuesday, November 10, 2026, The National reported, citing operator Adnoc Logistics and Services. In a statement, the company confirmed that response teams were working on the incident but did not publicly identify the ship, detail the damage or say whether anyone was injured. The incident comes amid a series of attacks and maritime incidents involving commercial shipping near the Strait of Hormuz. Reuters reported on November 11 that attacks on shipping were continuing and that uncertainty was affecting oil prices. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) also issued warnings about tanker incidents in the region, but those reports should not automatically be conflated with this particular UAE vessel attack.

The Crow's analysis

The incident adds to concerns about security along a major global oil and gas shipping route. Even without a complete closure, attacks can raise insurance, freight, escort and port operating costs. Energy markets have been volatile amid conflicting signals of possible US-Iran de-escalation and continued threats at sea. Reuters reported that oil futures rebounded on Wednesday after hitting two-month lows in the previous session. Confirmed damage reported by the operator must be distinguished from unverified claims about who was responsible or how the attack was carried out.

Counterpoint

The operator confirmed damage and a response effort but released no technical details establishing how the incident occurred, who carried it out or whether there were casualties. Iranian authorities have attributed some regional incidents to mines and shipping routes they regard as unauthorized, while other governments dispute those restrictions. Such statements do not establish Iranian responsibility for this particular incident. Further company or maritime authority statements may clarify the circumstances.
