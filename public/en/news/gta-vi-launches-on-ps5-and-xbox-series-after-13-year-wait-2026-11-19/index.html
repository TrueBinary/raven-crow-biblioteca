GTA VI LAUNCHES ON PS5 AND XBOX SERIES AFTER 13-YEAR WAIT

Rockstar confirms Grand Theft Auto VI's November 19 release, following Lucia and Jason through Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida.

What happened

Rockstar Games announced the worldwide release of Grand Theft Auto VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on Thursday, November 19, 2026. The story follows Lucia and Jason in Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida, inspired by Florida. The official announcement confirms availability on those consoles but does not announce a PC version for this date. Parent company Take-Two Interactive separately confirmed the release. The game arrives 13 years after Grand Theft Auto V, released in 2013.

The Crow's analysis

For consumers, the distinction between a confirmed release and the expectations built by trailers matters: Rockstar identifies specific platforms, and availability on other systems should not be assumed. Take-Two's June announcement set a starting US price of $79.99 and a $99.99 Ultimate edition, neither of which establishes an official price in Brazilian reais. The return to Vice City and the two-protagonist story are confirmed features. Any definitive commercial impact will depend on verifiable sales figures and sustained critical assessment, not launch-day excitement alone.

Counterpoint

Availability alone does not prove consistent technical performance, sales records or universal player satisfaction. Take-Two's promotional claim of exceptional launch reviews should be distinguished from independent, evolving assessments. Rockstar did not confirm a PC release date in this announcement. Official promotional images and trailers remain copyrighted and were not republished in this article.
