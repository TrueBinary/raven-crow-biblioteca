Palmeiras beat Vasco on penalties to reach Brazilian Cup final against Grêmio

A 2–1 defeat at São Januário was followed by a 5–4 shootout win, with Carlos Miguel saving two penalties.

What happened

Palmeiras reached the Copa do Brasil final on Sunday, November 8, 2026, by defeating Vasco 5–4 on penalties at São Januário stadium in Rio de Janeiro. Vasco won the match 2–1 in regulation time through goals from Colidio and Puma Rodríguez, while Piquerez scored for Palmeiras. Palmeiras had won the first leg 1–0, leaving the aggregate score level and sending the tie to a shootout. Goalkeeper Carlos Miguel saved attempts by Lescano and Andrés Gómez. Giay missed for Palmeiras, but Vitor Roque, Gustavo Gómez, Ramón Sosa, Andreas Pereira and Marlon Freitas scored their penalties. Palmeiras will face Grêmio in a one-off final on December 6, 2026, at Mané Garrincha stadium in Brasília. Folha de S.Paulo and UOL independently reported the qualification.

The Crow's analysis

The result highlights the distinction between winning a match and advancing in a two-legged knockout tie. Vasco overturned Palmeiras' advantage during regulation time but failed to complete the job in the shootout. Carlos Miguel's two saves were pivotal, yet all five successful Palmeiras penalties also mattered. The final against Grêmio will reprise the 2020 decider and offer Palmeiras another chance to lift the Copa do Brasil.

Counterpoint

Vasco's 2–1 regulation-time victory was not enough because Palmeiras had won the first leg 1–0. Vasco's performance should not be reduced to the elimination alone: the team forced the tie to penalties.
