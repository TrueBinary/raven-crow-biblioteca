Banco do Brasil's quarterly profit drops 66.5% as lender halves its 2026 forecast

Adjusted net profit falls to R$ 1.27 billion as agribusiness loan defaults push up credit costs.

What happened

On November 11, 2026, Banco do Brasil reported adjusted net profit of R$ 1.27 billion for the third quarter, down 66.5% from the same period of 2025. Accounting net profit was approximately R$ 1.22 billion. The state-controlled bank cut its full-year adjusted profit forecast from R$ 18 billion–R$ 22 billion to R$ 9 billion–R$ 11 billion. Quarterly credit costs rose 18.4% year on year to R$ 21.2 billion, while the ratio of loans overdue by more than 90 days increased to 6.08%, from 4.51% a year earlier. The expanded loan portfolio reached approximately R$ 1.3 trillion. The bank identified agribusiness loan defaults as a major source of pressure and reported R$ 8.6 billion in adjusted profit for the first nine months of 2026.

The Crow's analysis

The figures underscore mounting pressure on the profitability of a major agricultural lender. Halving the annual forecast suggests management expects a weaker finish to the year than previously projected. Higher credit costs and loan renegotiations may constrain growth and distributions. Adjusted earnings, accounting earnings and credit costs are distinct measures; the bank did not report a net loss for the quarter.

Counterpoint

Banco do Brasil says it is strengthening credit-risk management, restructuring troubled loans and recovering collateral while working to restore profitability. Weather-related crop losses, borrowing costs and commodity prices also affect farmers' ability to repay. One quarter's figures do not establish insolvency, misappropriation or responsibility by any particular politician.
