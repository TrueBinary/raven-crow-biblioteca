HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS RALLY IN PRAGUE AGAINST PUBLIC MEDIA FUNDING PLAN

Organizers estimate 350,000 to 450,000 demonstrators; Prime Minister Andrej Babiš denies that the plan threatens editorial independence.

What happened

Hundreds of thousands gathered in Prague's Letná Park on November 15, 2026, to oppose replacing household and business broadcasting fees with direct state-budget funding for public television and radio. Organizers estimated 350,000 to 450,000 participants; this is not an independently verified count. The proposal would reduce broadcasters' funding by around 15% and has passed its first parliamentary reading. The Milion chvilek movement organized the rally.

The Crow's analysis

Opponents fear that reliance on government budgets would expose public broadcasters to political pressure and force cuts in staff and programming. The rally came just before the anniversary of the 1989 Velvet Revolution, when Letná was also the scene of historic demonstrations.

Counterpoint

Prime Minister Andrej Babiš rejects claims that the proposal threatens press freedom, saying it would reduce household costs. The bill still requires further parliamentary approval and has not taken effect.
