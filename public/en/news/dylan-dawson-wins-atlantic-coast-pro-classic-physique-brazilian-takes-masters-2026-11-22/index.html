DYLAN DAWSON WINS ATLANTIC COAST PRO CLASSIC PHYSIQUE; BRAZILIAN TAKES MASTERS RUNNER-UP SPOT

South Africa's Dylan Dawson wins Classic Physique in Fort Lauderdale; Brazil's Rachid Souki finishes second in Masters 40+.

What happened

South African bodybuilder Dylan Dawson won the Open Classic Physique division at the Atlantic Coast Pro, held on November 21, 2026, in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, United States, according to the International Federation of Bodybuilding and Fitness Professional League (IFBB Pro League) official scorecard. The IFBB Pro League recorded Dawson first with a judging score of 3, ahead of runner-up Mudbi Favors with 6 and third-place Jarek Crew with 10. In Men's Bodybuilding Masters 40+, Brazilian competitor Rachid Souki placed second with 7, behind winner Troy Gardner with 3, according to the same official results.

The Crow's analysis

The official scorecards allow comparisons within each division, not direct comparisons between Classic Physique and Masters Bodybuilding competitors, whose judging standards and fields differ. Dawson's victory adds a professional international result, while Souki's runner-up finish is the confirmed Brazilian highlight in the Masters 40+ class. Organizers scheduled the event for November 21–22, with open and age-group divisions, making precise division labels essential.

Counterpoint
