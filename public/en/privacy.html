YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

Raven Crow respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you visit ravencrow.cloud, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Controller and contact

The controller of the personal data collected on this site is Raven Crow, an independent editorial project published at ravencrow.cloud. Questions, requests and complaints about privacy can be sent through our support channel on Instagram, @ravencrowcc.

2. Data we collect

We collect three types of information:

  • Browsing data: pages visited, visit duration, traffic source, device and browser type, and IP address.
  • Data you provide: your email, when you subscribe to the newsletter, and the information you send when you contact us.
  • Advertising and metrics data: statistical information and identifiers used to display and measure ads.

We don't require registration to read the site.

3. Purposes of processing

We use data to understand what readers look for and improve the site (legitimate interest), show ads that keep the project free (legitimate interest and consent, where required), send the newsletter you requested (consent), reply to messages, comply with legal obligations and keep the site secure (legitimate interest).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only with:

  • service providers that run the site, such as hosting, audience statistics, advertising and email delivery, who may use the data only to provide the contracted service;
  • public authorities, when there is a legal obligation or court order.

Some of these providers are located outside Brazil, and the transfer follows LGPD rules.

5. Data subject rights

Under the General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you may request confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent. We respond within 15 days. You may also appeal to the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use cookies to measure audience and display ads, and our advertising partners may use them to show ads based on your interests. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage Google personalized ads in ad settings. Blocking cookies does not prevent you from reading the site.

7. Security and storage

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and hosting services with protection against attacks. Data is stored in our service providers' systems, with restricted access.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for each purpose. Your newsletter email is removed from the list when you unsubscribe, with one click in any issue. Data may be kept longer when the law requires it.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of the page; we recommend checking it periodically.

10. Communication channel

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, contact us via @ravencrowcc on Instagram, stating your request and a way to reply.

11. Applicable law and jurisdiction

This policy follows Brazilian law, in particular the LGPD and the Marco Civil da Internet (Law 12.965/2014). Any disputes will be settled in the courts of the data subject's place of residence. See also the Terms of use.
